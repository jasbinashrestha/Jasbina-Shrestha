💖 My Crush Story 💖

It all started on 8 December 2022.

My friend and I had gone to another class to bring our friend who had brought a book for her. That day I saw him in his college uniform wearing a mask. His eyes looked so beautiful that I fell for him right then.

I stood beside my friends and secretly looked at him while listening to his sweet voice. By looking at him, I felt that he was my type and I loved that feeling.

That same day I asked my friend his name, searched for his Instagram ID, and sent him a friend request… but he didn’t accept it. After a while, I canceled the request.

A few days later I went to another section to borrow a math book and asked him for it, but he didn’t give it to me. I returned to my class and asked my friend instead, and she gave the book to me.

At that moment I felt that maybe I first needed to become his friend. Our classes were different — I was a biology student and he was a computer student.

So I could only see him during break time or when passing by the corridor. Days passed like that and no one ever knew he was my crush. I secretly watched him from outside the classroom window.

He was talented in math just like me, and I liked that about him. Sometimes I felt maybe we were alike… and that thought always made me smile.

In grade 11 the days passed without me talking to him because I believed he was an introvert and I was too.

Later in grade 12 I heard that he had a girlfriend. At that moment I lost all hope and thought maybe I would never have him.

Still, he remained my first crush and my first love.

During sports week I participated in badminton and basketball, while he played chess, volleyball and basketball. I think basketball and badminton were his favorite sports too.

Soon our grade 12 exams came and everyone got busy. During the exams I didn’t see him at all.

After the exams, we finally met again at our farewell.

He was wearing a black coat, pants and shirt… and he looked so handsome that I can’t even describe it in words.

That day we took a picture together. My friend even told us to hold hands so the photo would look nice. For me, that moment felt very special.

After that we never met again. I heard he was planning to go to Australia, but in the end he went to another country.

One random day I searched for his Facebook again and sent him a friend request… and this time he accepted it.

Now he is in my friend list. Even though he rarely posts anything, I can still see small updates about him.

Maybe someday in the future we will meet again.

✨ Until then, life goes on and I keep moving forward, focusing on myself and my own path. ✨
