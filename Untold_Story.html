My Untold Story

A story I never had the courage to tell.

Chapter 1 — The First Time I Saw Him

It all started on December 9th, 2022. My friend and I had gone to another class to pick up some yomari that her friend had brought for her. That day, I saw him in his college uniform, wearing a mask. His eyes looked so beautiful that I fell for him right then and there.

While they were talking, I just stood beside them, secretly looking at him and listening to his sweet voice. Looking at him, I felt like he was my type. Yes, he was. I loved that feeling.

That same day, I asked my friend for his name, searched for his Instagram ID, and sent him a friend request. However, he didn't accept it, so after a while, I cancelled the request.

Chapter 2 — Small Moments

A few days later, I went to another section to borrow a math book. I asked him for it, but he didn't give it to me. Feeling upset, I returned to my class and messaged my friend instead; she asked him, and he gave the book to her.

At that moment, I felt like maybe I first needed to become his friend. Since our classes were different—I was a biology student and he was a computer science student —I could only see him during breaks or tiffin time.

Days passed by just like that, but no one ever knew he was my crush. I secretly used to watch him from the classroom window.

He was talented in math, just like me, and I liked that about him. I often thought to myself, "Maybe we are alike," and the thought made me smile.

Chapter 3 — The Distance

In Grade 11, the days went by without me talking to him because I believed he was an introvert, and I was too.

Then, in Grade 12, I heard that he had a girlfriend. I lost all hope, thinking, "Maybe I will never have him."

After that, I focused on myself. Still, I kept thinking about him because he was my first crush, my first love.

I wanted to tell him how much I loved him, but I couldn't. He had a girlfriend now, and I didn't want to interfere in their relationship.

Chapter 4 — Things I Never Said

One day, while I was walking home with my friend, she told me that he had once confessed his love to her. I was shocked, thinking to myself, "What? He used to like her?"

But on the outside, I just laughed at her words.

Little by little, I came to know more things about him, and each time, I felt bad for myself.

I often thought that if I had confessed to him in the beginning, maybe he would have been with me now, but I couldn't.

Chapter 5 — Sports Week

During sports week, I participated in badminton and basketball. My crush took part in chess, volleyball, and I think basketball and badminton as well.

None of my friends were interested in playing games, so I was the only one who joined the events.

Since I didn't want to be alone, I also encouraged my friend to participate.

On the day I had my games, he didn't have any matches, so I couldn't see him.

After sports week ended, I heard that he had won his game. I felt really happy for him, but I didn't get the chance to talk to him.

Chapter 6 — The Farewell

Soon, our Grade 12 exams were approaching, and everyone was busy preparing. During the exams, I didn't get to see him at all.

After the exams, I finally saw him at our farewell. He was wearing a black coat, pants, and a shirt.

He looked so handsome that I can't even describe it in words.

That day, I told my friend indirectly that I wanted to click a photo with the boy who was wearing the perfect dress.

She called him over—he was my crush—and we took a picture together.

It was perfect. We were even holding hands because my friend told us to do so the picture would look better.

At that time, I had also heard rumors that he had broken up with his girlfriend.

Chapter 7 — Life Moves On

After that, we never saw each other again.

Whenever I met up with my friends, I used to ask about him indirectly.

Later, I heard that he was planning to go to Australia, but in the end he went to another country.

I don't know why he chose a different country, but I am happy for him and his future ahead.

One random day, I searched for his Facebook ID again and sent him a friend request. He accepted.

Now he is on my friend list, so I can still get updates about him, although he never really posts anything.

Final Chapter — A Hope

I still hope that in the future we will meet again and have a chance to get together.

But for now, life is moving on.

I am just focusing on myself — no crush, no distraction, just me and my future.

— From someone who once secretly admired you ❤️
